About

That's what I try to do in every project. From an idea and imagination — create something that can be felt.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is collage animation?

Collage animation is a style where photos, textures, art objects and video are combined into surreal moving visuals. It creates a unique aesthetic that stands out on social media and works well for lifestyle, psychology, astrology and personal brands.

What types of animation do you create?

I create three styles: collage animation, 2D vector animation with clean characters and scenes, and 2D character animation with hand-drawn personality. I also produce animated product cards and AI-generated visual content.

How do I order animation for my brand?

Fill out the contact form on this page or write to me directly. Tell me about your project, the format you need and your timeline. I respond within 1–2 days.

What tools do you use?

I work primarily in Alight Motion for 2D and collage animation. For AI-generated content I use the latest image and video generation tools.

Can you create animation for social media — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube?

Yes. I create animated content optimised for all major platforms — Reels, TikTok videos, YouTube Shorts, stories and feed posts.
