About

I am Nat.

I'm a self-taught animator and collage artist. No courses, no teachers — just me, the screen, and the universe of ideas.

WACD stands for We Are Cosmic Dust. Everything we are and everything we create comes from stardust. That's the idea behind every frame I make.

I work with animation, collage, and illustration — building worlds frame by frame, from nothing.
